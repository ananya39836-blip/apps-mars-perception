## 4. Terramechanics & Mobility Performance Analysis

Evaluated using the Bekker-Wong terramechanics formulation:
$$p = \left(\frac{k_c}{b} + k_\phi\right) z^n$$
$$\tau(x) = \left(c + p(x)\tan\phi\right)\left(1 - e^{-j(x)/K}\right)$$
$$\text{DP} = F_{thrust} - R_c - R_b$$

### Performance Across Standard Planetary Soil Types:
- **Firm Soil / Bedrock**:
  - Sinkage $z_0 < 0.003\text{ m}$
  - Peak Drawbar Pull: $+118.4\text{ N}$ per wheel
  - Net Vehicle DP ($6$ wheels): $+710.4\text{ N}$ (Excellent traction)
- **Medium Sand (Meridiani Planum)**:
  - Sinkage $z_0 \approx 0.012\text{ m}$
  - Compaction Resistance $R_c \approx 4.8\text{ N}$
  - Net Vehicle DP: $+512.6\text{ N}$ (Nominal operational corridor)
- **Soft Drift Sand (Sand Dunes)**:
  - Sinkage $z_0 \approx 0.038\text{ m}$
  - Compaction Resistance $R_c \approx 16.4\text{ N}$, Bulldozing Resistance $R_b \approx 8.2\text{ N}$
  - Net Vehicle DP: $+194.2\text{ N}$ (Reduced safety margin; slip monitoring critical)
- **Loose Regolith / Dust Mantle**:
  - Sinkage $z_0 \approx 0.046\text{ m}$
  - Compaction Resistance $R_c \approx 22.1\text{ N}$, Bulldozing Resistance $R_b \approx 11.5\text{ N}$
  - Net Vehicle DP: $+112.8\text{ N}$ (Watchdog limits forward speed to $v \le 0.05\text{ m/s}$)
